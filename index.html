How it works

  1. List what you can make

    Add your machines, materials and skills to your profile.

  2. Post a job

    Upload your files and say what you need, how many and by when.

  3. Get quotes

    Members with the right machines reply with price and lead time.

  4. Get it made

    Pay materials at cost, a little for machine time and a small handling fee. Pick up from a maker nearby, or have it shipped anywhere in the US.

Why Maker Network

Makers helping makers

Every member is a maker. You make what your machines are good at, and others make what you can't.

At cost, no markup

Makers charge materials at cost plus set machine-time rates and a small handling fee. No profit on jobs.

No cut of your jobs

A flat $5 a month (or $50 a year) covers running costs. Earn points for every job to spend on spare materials and perks.
